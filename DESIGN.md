# Design System: BAP Community Service

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Service Passbook"**

The semester is a passbook. Every event a student serves leaves a crimson stamp on the page until the page is full; everything else is ink on paper. The colours are the chapter crest's: a barely-beige paper ground, white entries framed in 1px khaki rules, charcoal ink on a warm ramp, and crimson reserved for what acts, what is next, and what was earned. Grey grouped-list chrome and dashboards of tinted cards are rejected; three quiet chrome values (ground, white entry, khaki rule) and one crimson carry every screen.

Hierarchy is scale, not decoration. One family, Hanken Grotesk, runs the whole interface on a steep ramp where the points figure (64px, 800) is the only large type; Cinzel appears only inside stamps, where an inked seal is the native material. Time is drawn to length: the event pass shows the time on site as a bar with the check-out moment marked. The crest seal recurs at three sizes (the bar, a met requirement, sign-in) and is the browser-tab and home-screen icon.

Students use it on phones on the day; the committee works the same ink-and-khaki ledger on phones and laptops. Density is comfortable and phone-first, flat, and calm. There is exactly one authored motion: the check-in or check-out stamp landing on the entry.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Paper ground, white entries, 1px khaki frames, warm hairlines between rows; no grey chrome, no tinted card grounds.
- Crimson only for actions, the next (or today's) date, stamps, alerts and focus.
- State hues (green, amber, blue, gold) appear small: dots, short status words and signed figures in ink-on-white pills, notices and tables; never as fills.
- Hanken Grotesk on a steep role ramp with tabular figures; the 64px points figure is the only large type; Cinzel only in stamps.
- The stamp grid and the event pass are the signature surfaces; the stamp landing is the only authored animation.
- Inline two-step confirmation; never the browser's confirm() dialog.

## Colors

Crest colours on paper: a warm neutral ramp framed in khaki, with one crimson and a handful of small state hues.

### Primary
- **Crest Crimson** (crimson): primary pill buttons, plain text actions, the back link, the "Sign in" link, the nav count badge, the switch's on state, the focus ring and caret, error lines and alert notices, the next event's (or today's) date box, and every stamp. **Pressed Crimson** (crimson-hover) is primary hover; **Crimson Blush** (crimson-soft) is the hover ground behind plain buttons and the text-selection highlight. Text on crimson is **On Crimson** (on-crimson).

### Secondary
- **Requirement Gold** (gold, gold-bright): earned only. Gold-bright fills the points progress bar once the requirement is met and is the dot of the exception ("sand") pill; gold is the text of a met total in the committee's points table.

### Tertiary (state)
- **Confirmed Green** (green): checked in, confirmed, positive deltas.
- **Burnt Amber** (amber): warnings, late drops, early check-outs, no-shows, negative deltas.
- **Notice Blue** (blue): neutral system status (signed up, sign-ups open, informational notices).
All three appear as a 6–8px dot, a short status word, a line icon or a signed figure on white; never as a tinted ground.

### Neutral
- **Charcoal Ink** (text): all primary text; the time-on-site check-out tick.
- **Khaki Secondary** (secondary): subtitles, footnotes, field labels, inactive nav, the "of 12 points" beside the figure.
- **Placeholder Khaki** (placeholder): every field placeholder.
- **Faint Khaki** (tertiary): chevrons and empty-state line icons only; never readable text.
- **Crest Khaki** (rule): the 1px frame on every entry, pill, field, date box and stamp box; the header's bottom rule; the dashed empty stamp box and pending stamp; the time-on-site span; the scrollbar.
- **Warm Hairline** (separator): between rows inside an entry.
- **Parchment Fill** (fill) and **Parchment Strong** (fill-strong): hover and press tint on rows and secondary buttons, the progress and time-on-site track, the segmented track, avatar ground, the struck-box hatch; fill-strong is the switch's off track.
- **Paper Ground** (ground): the page behind everything and the header (at 95%); also the theme colour.
- **Entry White** (surface): every entry, panel, pass, field, pill, date box and stamp box.

### Named Rules
**The Three Chrome Values Rule.** Chrome is paper ground, white entry and a 1px khaki rule. Depth, grouping and emphasis come from those three; do not add grey grounds, tinted card grounds or shadows to say "this is a group".

**The Crimson Burns Once Rule.** Crimson means act here, this is next, or this was stamped. Exactly one crimson-filled primary per view; only the next event's (or today's) date box is crimson; nothing decorative, no headings, no category colour.

**The Small State Rule.** State colour is carried by a dot, a short word or a figure in ink-on-white, never by a filled or tinted block.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Hanken Grotesk (via next/font, with -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Hanken Grotesk
**Seal Font:** Cinzel 700, inside stamps only

**Character:** A warm grotesk doing all the work through size and weight on one family, tightened slightly (-0.006em on body to -0.04em on the figure), with tabular figures set on the body so points, times and counts align. Cinzel is the stamp's engraved ink, not a heading face.

### Hierarchy
Roles are defined once as theme tokens in `globals.css`; screens use the role, not a literal size.
- **Figure** (800, 64px, 0.9, -0.04em): the student's points total, and nothing else.
- **Display** (700, 30px, 1.15): page titles (the committee term title, the event console, sign-in).
- **Title** (700, 20px, 1.25): section headings ("Open for sign-up", "History", "Next"), the event title on the pass.
- **Headline** (600, 17px, 1.3): the wordmark beside the seal, "of 12 points", status titles, large buttons, struck-box figures.
- **Body** (400; 500 for row titles; 15px, 1.45): row titles, inputs, notices, pass metadata, prose.
- **Footnote** (13px, 1.4): row subtitles, field labels and hints, the time-on-site labels, small buttons and segments.
- **Caption** (600, 12px): pill labels and nav count badges.
- **Seal** (Cinzel 700, sized to the stamp by container units, 0.06em tracking): signed points, IN/OUT, time and date inside stamps; uppercase is native here.
The date box's month (700, 10px, uppercase) and day number (17/20/28px) are its own fixed sizes.

### Named Rules
**The One Large Number Rule.** The points figure is the only large type on any screen; everything else lives at 30px or below.

**The Seal Stays In The Stamp Rule.** Cinzel appears only inside stamps; never for headings, the wordmark or labels.

**The Tabular Rule.** Every number renders with tabular figures; it is set on the body, so do not turn it off.

## Layout

One 1024px frame (max-w-5xl) for every signed-in page, header and content alike, with 16px gutters that respect the landscape safe area, so the bar and content edge never move between sections. Phones get one column. From 1024px the student home and event page split into two columns at 5:7 with a 40px gap: on home, the figure, stamp grid and next pass on the left (sticky under the bar), lists on the right. Committee forms (new and edit event) sit left-aligned at 672px inside the frame. Sign-in, privacy, 404 and error are standalone centred pages with no bar; the on-site QR screen is a standalone full-height display.

A sticky 56px bar on the paper ground (95%) carries the seal and "Community Service" on the left and section links and the avatar on the right, closed by a 1px khaki rule. Committee screens add a horizontally scrolling subnav of pill links that fades at the phone's right edge. Content starts 24px under the bar and clears the bottom safe area.

Rhythm is section-based: 32px between sections, 10px between a section heading and its entry, 8px to a footer. Rows are at least 56px with 12px/16px padding and 12px between leading box, text and trailing content. Panels and passes pad 16px, 20px from 640px. The stamp grid is four columns with 8px gaps.

On touch screens every small control gets a 44px target through the `.hit` class (an invisible expanded hit area, look unchanged); segmented controls and selects grow to 40/44px; fields render at 16px on coarse pointers so iOS never zooms on focus. On phones a row's trailing status stacks under its subtitle; page-title actions wrap below the title.

**The Next Thing First Rule.** Every screen opens with its title or figure and then the single most relevant object (the stamp grid and next pass, "Needs attention"), followed by lists. Never open with a grid of summary cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth is the khaki rule: white entries sit on paper and are separated from it by a 1px frame, not a shadow. The event pass is flat like everything else. Shadows exist only on things that physically float or move.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Menu float** (`0 12px 32px rgb(46 46 46 / 0.12)` with a khaki ring): the account menu and the console's action menus.
- **Control thumb** (`0 1px 2px rgb(46 46 46 / 0.1)` on the selected segment; `0 2px 4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.18)` on the switch knob): native-control affordance.
- **Stamp inner ring** (`inset 0 0 0 3px surface, inset 0 0 0 4.5px crimson`): draws the stamp's second ring; ink, not elevation.

### Named Rules
**The Rule Not Shadow Rule.** Entries, panels, passes, notices and stamp boxes never cast a shadow. If a new surface needs separation, frame it in khaki.

## Shapes

Soft corners scaled to the object: 8px on segments and menu items, 10px on stamp boxes and the segmented track, 12px on fields, selects and date boxes, 16px on entries, panels, passes, notices, empty states and menus. Everything pressed as a discrete action (buttons, pills, nav links, switches, avatars, progress bars) is fully round, and so is the stamp. Frames are always 1px khaki; the stamp is a 2px crimson ring with an inner hairline ring. Dashed khaki means "still to fill" (empty stamp boxes, the pending stamp, empty states); a 135° hatch in fill means a struck box (no-show or negative adjustment). Icons are 2px-stroke line icons (Lucide), 16px in metadata. The crest seal is a raster image, never redrawn.

## Components

### Buttons
Pills; one loud one per view.
- **Shape:** fully round at 32px (small), 40px (medium) and 50px (large, full width on the pass).
- **Primary:** crimson fill, white semibold label; deepens on hover.
- **Secondary:** white with a 1px inset khaki ring and ink label; fill tint on hover. "Open to check out" and "Cancel" use it.
- **Plain:** crimson label, no fill; blush on hover. Inline row actions and the unarmed confirm.
- **Destructive:** white with a 40% crimson ring and crimson label; the armed state of a destructive confirmation.
- **States:** 150ms background transition, press scales to 98%, disabled at 40% opacity, 2px crimson focus outline offset 2px. Small buttons carry `.hit`.

### Inline Confirmation
A plain button that, when tapped, swaps in place for Cancel (secondary) and the confirm label (destructive, or primary when not destructive), focuses the confirm, and disarms after 5 seconds. It replaces every browser confirm() dialog.

### Pills and Notices
- **Pill:** 24px, white ground, 1px inset khaki ring, 12px semibold ink label, with a 6px state dot (crimson, green, amber, blue, gold-bright). Neutral pills drop the dot and use secondary text.
- **Notice:** a 16px-radius white block with a khaki ring, 15px ink text and an 8px leading state dot; an optional action stacks under it on phones. A crimson notice is an alert: crimson text and a 50% crimson ring.

### Entries / Containers
- **Entry (group):** white, 16px radius, 1px khaki ring, rows divided by warm hairlines, no shadow.
- **Row:** 56px minimum; optional leading date box or avatar, 15px medium title, 13px secondary subtitle, trailing status or signed figure, faint-khaki chevron when it links. Linked rows tint with fill on hover and press.
- **Panel:** the same frame with 16/20px padding, for forms and composite content.
- **Empty:** a dashed khaki block, centred faint line icon, 15px semibold title and secondary explanation.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white ground, 1px inset khaki ring, 12px radius, 15px ink text, placeholder khaki. Selects draw their own chevron in secondary.
- **Focus:** the ring thickens to 2px crimson at 60%.
- **Field:** 13px medium secondary label above; hint below in secondary, replaced by a crimson error line.
- **Toggle:** a 51 × 31px switch, fill-strong off, crimson on, white knob.

### Navigation
- **Bar:** the 30px crest seal and "Community Service" (17px bold) on the left; "My service" / "Committee" links and the avatar menu on the right; 1px khaki rule beneath.
- **Nav links and subnav:** 32px pills, 15px medium secondary; active is a white pill with a khaki ring and ink semibold label. A crimson count badge marks items needing attention. The subnav keeps the active pill in view.
- **Segmented control:** parchment track, white selected segment with a khaki ring and thumb shadow, 13px semibold.
- **Back link:** crimson 15px label with a line chevron.

### Date Box
A printed-calendar box: white, 12px radius, 1px khaki ring, 10px uppercase month over the day number, at 40/48/64px. The next event's box (and today's) switches its ring and type to crimson; never a crimson fill. Past events grey the day.

### Stamp Grid (signature)
The passbook page under the points figure: four columns of 10px-radius khaki-framed boxes, one per scored event. A served event carries a crimson double-ring Cinzel stamp (signed points over the date) at 76% of the box, tilted on a fixed cycle (-9°, 5°, -3°, 8°, -6°). A no-show or negative adjustment is struck with the fill hatch and a secondary figure and reason. Points awaiting the committee show a dashed khaki "Pending" stamp; boxes still needed before the requirement are dashed and empty.

### Event Pass (signature)
The event as a passbook entry: a khaki-framed white entry with the 64px date box beside the 20px title, time, place and partner; then the time on site drawn to scale (a fill track with the required stretch in khaki and an ink tick labelled "Check out from …"); a status line with a state dot; then one full-width large action (Check in, or the secondary Open to check out). Used on home ("Next") and atop the event page.

### Check-in Stamp
On the event page, attendance shows as an 80px crimson stamp reading IN or OUT with the time and date, rested at -9°. Right after the tap it lands once: `stamp-land`, 420ms, ease-out-expo (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), from 1.9× scale and -24° to rest. Under reduced motion it appears already at rest. This is the only authored animation; everything else moves through 150–200ms state transitions.

### Crest Seal
The chapter crest raster: 30px in the bar, 56px beside the figure once the requirement is met (and on the QR screen), 96px on sign-in, and the favicon and Apple touch icon. Always decorative next to words that name the app.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** frame every entry, panel, pass, field, pill and date box in a 1px khaki (rule) ring on white, on the paper ground.
- **Do** put exactly one crimson-filled primary in a view; every other action is a secondary khaki-ringed pill or a plain crimson label.
- **Do** mark only the next event's (or today's) date box in crimson ring and type, never a crimson fill.
- **Do** carry state with a small dot, word or signed figure in green, amber, blue or gold on white.
- **Do** keep the 64px points figure the only large type, and Cinzel inside stamps only.
- **Do** use dashed khaki for what is still to fill and the hatch for what was struck.
- **Do** confirm risky actions inline with the two-step Confirm Button (Cancel / confirm label, 5-second disarm).
- **Do** give small controls `.hit` and keep fields at 16px on coarse pointers.
- **Do** respect reduced motion on the stamp landing.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use grey grouped grounds, a dark mode, or the system font stack as the display face; this world is light paper only.
- **Don't** fill or tint a surface with a state colour (no green, amber or blue soft-tinted pills, notices or cards).
- **Don't** put shadows on entries, panels, passes or stamp boxes; shadows are for floating menus and control thumbs.
- **Don't** add a second authored animation or decorative motion.
- **Don't** use crimson for headings, decoration or category colour.
- **Don't** put small uppercase kickers or eyebrows above titles; uppercase belongs to the date box month and the stamp's ink.
- **Don't** set readable text in the faint khaki (tertiary); it is for chevrons and empty-state icons.
- **Don't** use the browser's confirm() or alert() dialogs.
